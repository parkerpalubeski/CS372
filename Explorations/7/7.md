## What’s the difference between a hub and a switch?
Hubs transmit packets to all devices on a network, kind of like a broadcast address. A switch on the other hand transmits ethernet packets only to the device that its destined for.

## What does a router do?
Routers consult routing table to forward traffic to its destination.

## Why would a router with only one network connection not make any sense?
Because a router is fundamentally designed to connect multiple networks. The way I think of it is that having a router with only one connection is like placing a 4-way intersection complete with lights and everything right on a dead-end section of road.
