1. Get your MAC address.

In Windows at the (non-WSL) command prompt, you can probably find it with:

getmac
or

ipconfig /all
On a Mac or Linux if you have the IP tools installed, you can run:

ip addr
If not, on a Mac, you can find it in System Settings→Network→Advanced.

```
90:e8:68:77:71:67
```

3. Click a packet at random in the timeline. If you don't see any, maybe you chose the wrong device.

4. Is your MAC the source, the destination, or neither?

5. What is the remote MAC address that is listed for this packet? (i.e. the MAC address that isn't yours.)

6. What kind of (probably partial) packet is wrapped up in this Ethernet frame, that is, what inner protocols are wrapped up in it?

7. If this isn't a TCP or UDP packet, choose a different one until you get one.

8. What are the source and destination ports?
